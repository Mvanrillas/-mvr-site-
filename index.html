What is the difference between the Take-off and Fit for Growth?

The Take-off tells you what to fix and gets your first moves started, in 3 to 4 weeks at a fixed price. Fit for Growth designs and puts in place the solutions to your priorities. It is a separate decision, with its own proposal.

What if I do not want to continue after the Take-off?

Then you stop there, with your priorities, your map, your quick wins and the 30-day call. Many leaders do.

How much of my time does it take?

About four hours over the whole Take-off: the owner interview, the 90-minute working session, the debrief and the check-in call.

What is “the one question that matters to me”?

The thing you most want an answer to right now. For example: why do decisions keep coming back to me? Or: are my managers set up to run their teams? We agree it on the discovery call, and the Take-off is built around it.

How does payment work?

The Take-off is 3,000 EUR excl. VAT, 50% at acceptance and 50% at the final report. Fit for Growth is priced on scope and billed in stages.

Is this only for companies with an HR team?

No. Most of the companies I work with have no HR team, or one person doing HR alongside another job. The work is with you and your managers.

Can we work in French or English?

Both. Interviews, sessions and the report can be in either language, or a mix if your team is.
